# Article 22: what changed at each stage


## Secretariat outline (E/CN.4/AC.1/3) → Cassin draft (E/CN.4/AC.1/W.2/Rev.2)

- 1. **added**: “to the utmost of its possibilities” (… right to social security to the utmost of its possibilities the state shall maintain …)
- 2. **replaced**: “for” → “with the participation of beneficiaries shall organise” (… prevention of unemployment and with the participation of beneficiaries shall organise insurance against invalidity illness …)
- 3. **replaced**: “the risks of unemployment accident disability sickness” → “invalidity illness” (… shall organise insurance against invalidity illness old age and all …)
- 4. **added**: “all” (… illness old age and all other involuntary and undeserved …)
- 5. **replaced**: “or” → “and” (… and all other involuntary and undeserved losses of livelihood …)
- 6. **replaced**: “loss” → “losses” (… other involuntary and undeserved losses of livelihood mothers and …)
- 7. **added**: “mothers and children have the right to special regard care and resources” (… undeserved losses of livelihood mothers and children have the right to special regard care and resources …)

## Cassin draft (E/CN.4/AC.1/W.2/Rev.2) → Drafting Committee (E/CN.4/21)

- 1. **replaced**: “maintain effective arrangements” → “undertake measures” (… possibilities the state shall undertake measures for the promotion of …)
- 2. **replaced**: “prevention” → “promotion” (… undertake measures for the promotion of full employment and …)
- 3. **added**: “full employment and for the security of the individual against” (… for the promotion of full employment and for the security of the individual against unemployment disability old age …)
- 4. **replaced**: “and with the participation of beneficiaries shall organise insurance against invalidity illness” → “disability” (… the individual against unemployment disability old age and all …)
- 5. **replaced**: “involuntary and undeserved losses” → “loss” (… age and all other loss of livelihood for reasons …)
- 6. **added**: “for reasons beyond his control” (… other loss of livelihood for reasons beyond his control mothers and children have …)

## Drafting Committee (E/CN.4/21) → Geneva working group (E/CN.4/57)

- 1. **replaced**: “every one” → “everyone” (… everyone has the right to …)
- 2. **dropped**: “to the utmost of its possibilities” (… right to social security to the utmost of its possibilities the state shall undertake …)
- 3. **replaced**: “shall undertake” → “has a duty to maintain or ensure the maintenance of comprehensive” (… social security the state has a duty to maintain or ensure the maintenance of comprehensive measures for the security …)
- 4. **dropped**: “for the promotion of full employment and” (… state shall undertake measures for the promotion of full employment and for the security of …)
- 5. **added**: “the consequences of” (… of the individual against the consequences of unemployment disability old age …)
- 6. **replaced**: “mothers” → “motherhood shall be granted special care” (… reasons beyond his control motherhood shall be granted special care and assistance children are …)
- 7. **added**: “assistance” (… granted special care and assistance children are similarly entitled …)
- 8. **replaced**: “have the right” → “are similarly entitled” (… care and assistance children are similarly entitled to special care and …)
- 9. **dropped**: “regard” (… the right to special regard care and resources …)
- 10. **replaced**: “resources” → “assistance” (… to special care and assistance …)

## Geneva working group (E/CN.4/57) → Commission, 2nd session (E/600)

- 1. **replaced**: “everyone” → “every one” (… every one has the right to …)
- 2. **replaced**: “consequences” → “consequence” (… the individual against the consequence of unemployment disability old …)

## Commission, 2nd session (E/600) → Drafting Committee, 2nd session (E/CN.4/95)

No change.


## Drafting Committee, 2nd session (E/CN.4/95) → Commission, 3rd session (E/800)

- 1. **replaced**: “every one” → “everyone as a member of society” (… everyone as a member of society has the right to …)
- 2. **added**: “and is entitled to” (… right to social security and is entitled to the realization through national …)
- 3. **added**: “realization through national effort and international co-operation and in accordance with the organization and resources of each” (… is entitled to the realization through national effort and international co-operation and in accordance with the organization and resources of each state of the economic …)
- 4. **dropped**: “has a duty to maintain or ensure the maintenance of comprehensive measures for the security” (… social security the state has a duty to maintain or ensure the maintenance of comprehensive measures for the security of the individual against …)
- 5. **replaced**: “individual against the consequence of unemployment disability old age” → “economic social” (… each state of the economic social and cultural rights set …)
- 6. **replaced**: “all other loss of livelihood for reasons beyond his control motherhood shall be granted special care and assistance children are similarly entitled to special care and assistance” → “cultural rights set out below” (… the economic social and cultural rights set out below …)

## Commission, 3rd session (E/800) → Third Committee (A/777)

- 1. **dropped**: “the” (… and is entitled to the realization through national effort …)
- 2. **replaced**: “set out below” → “indispensable for his dignity and the free development of his personality” (… social and cultural rights indispensable for his dignity and the free development of his personality …)

## Third Committee (A/777) → Adopted (A/RES/217(III))

No change.

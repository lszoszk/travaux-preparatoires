# Article 12: what changed at each stage


## Secretariat outline (E/CN.4/AC.1/3) → Cassin draft (E/CN.4/AC.1/W.2/Rev.2)

- 1. **replaced**: “no one shall be subjected to arbitrary searches or seizures or to unreasonable interference with his person” → “the inviolability of privacy” (… the inviolability of privacy home correspondence and reputation …)
- 2. **replaced**: “family relations” → “correspondence and” (… inviolability of privacy home correspondence and reputation are inviolable and …)
- 3. **replaced**: “privacy activities or personal property the secrecy of correspondence shall be respected” → “are inviolable and protected by law” (… home correspondence and reputation are inviolable and protected by law …)

## Cassin draft (E/CN.4/AC.1/W.2/Rev.2) → Drafting Committee (E/CN.4/21)

- 1. **replaced**: “inviolability” → “privacy” (… the privacy of the home and …)
- 2. **replaced**: “privacy” → “the” (… the privacy of the home and of correspondence …)
- 3. **added**: “and of” (… privacy of the home and of correspondence and respect for …)
- 4. **added**: “respect for” (… and of correspondence and respect for reputation shall be protected …)
- 5. **replaced**: “are inviolable and” → “shall be” (… and respect for reputation shall be protected by law …)

## Drafting Committee (E/CN.4/21) → Geneva working group (E/CN.4/57)

- 1. **replaced**: “the” → “everyone shall be entitled to protection under law from unreasonable interference with his reputation his” (… everyone shall be entitled to protection under law from unreasonable interference with his reputation his privacy and his family …)
- 2. **replaced**: “of the” → “and his family his” (… his reputation his privacy and his family his home and correspondence shall …)
- 3. **dropped**: “of” (… of the home and of correspondence and respect for …)
- 4. **dropped**: “and respect for reputation” (… home and of correspondence and respect for reputation shall be protected by …)
- 5. **replaced**: “protected by law” → “inviolable” (… and correspondence shall be inviolable …)

## Geneva working group (E/CN.4/57) → Commission, 2nd session (E/600)

- 1. **replaced**: “everyone” → “every one” (… every one shall be entitled to …)

## Commission, 2nd session (E/600) → Drafting Committee, 2nd session (E/CN.4/95)

- 1. **replaced**: “shall be” → “is” (… every one is entitled to protection under …)
- 2. **added**: “the” (… entitled to protection under the law from unreasonable interference …)
- 3. **dropped**: “his” (… from unreasonable interference with his reputation his privacy and …)
- 4. **dropped**: “his privacy and his” (… interference with his reputation his privacy and his family his home and …)
- 5. **dropped**: “his” (… privacy and his family his home and correspondence shall …)
- 6. **replaced**: “and” → “or” (… with reputation family home or correspondence …)
- 7. **dropped**: “shall be inviolable” (… his home and correspondence shall be inviolable …)

## Drafting Committee, 2nd session (E/CN.4/95) → Commission, 3rd session (E/800)

- 1. **replaced**: “every” → “no” (… no one shall be subjected …)
- 2. **replaced**: “is entitled” → “shall be subjected” (… no one shall be subjected to unreasonable interference with …)
- 3. **dropped**: “protection under the law from” (… one is entitled to protection under the law from unreasonable interference with reputation …)
- 4. **replaced**: “reputation” → “his privacy” (… to unreasonable interference with his privacy family home correspondence or …)
- 5. **added**: “correspondence” (… his privacy family home correspondence or reputation …)
- 6. **replaced**: “correspondence” → “reputation” (… family home correspondence or reputation …)

## Commission, 3rd session (E/800) → Third Committee (A/777)

- 1. **replaced**: “unreasonable” → “arbitrary” (… shall be subjected to arbitrary interference with his privacy …)
- 2. **added**: “or” (… his privacy family home or correspondence nor to attacks …)
- 3. **added**: “nor to attacks upon his honour and reputation everyone has the right to the protection of the law against such interference” (… family home or correspondence nor to attacks upon his honour and reputation everyone has the right to the protection of the law against such interference or attacks …)
- 4. **replaced**: “reputation” → “attacks” (… against such interference or attacks …)

## Third Committee (A/777) → Adopted (A/RES/217(III))

No change.

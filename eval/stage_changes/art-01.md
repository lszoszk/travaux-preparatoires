# Article 1: what changed at each stage


## Cassin draft (E/CN.4/AC.1/W.2/Rev.2) → Drafting Committee (E/CN.4/21)

- 1. **added**: “and conscience they are” (… being endowed with reason and conscience they are members of one family …)

## Drafting Committee (E/CN.4/21) → Geneva working group (E/CN.4/57)

- 1. **replaced**: “brothers being” → “born free and equal in dignity and rights they are” (… all men are born free and equal in dignity and rights they are endowed by nature with …)
- 2. **added**: “by nature” (… rights they are endowed by nature with reason and conscience …)
- 3. **replaced**: “they are members of” → “and should act towards” (… with reason and conscience and should act towards one another like brothers …)
- 4. **replaced**: “family they are free and possess equal dignity and rights” → “another like brothers” (… should act towards one another like brothers …)

## Geneva working group (E/CN.4/57) → Commission, 2nd session (E/600)

No change.


## Commission, 2nd session (E/600) → Drafting Committee, 2nd session (E/CN.4/95)

No change.


## Drafting Committee, 2nd session (E/CN.4/95) → Commission, 3rd session (E/800)

- 1. **replaced**: “men” → “human beings” (… all human beings are born free and …)
- 2. **replaced**: “like brothers” → “in a spirit of brotherhood” (… act towards one another in a spirit of brotherhood …)

## Commission, 3rd session (E/800) → Third Committee (A/777)

- 1. **dropped**: “by nature” (… rights they are endowed by nature with reason and conscience …)

## Third Committee (A/777) → Adopted (A/RES/217(III))

No change.

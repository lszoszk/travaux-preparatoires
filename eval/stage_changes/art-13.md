# Article 13: what changed at each stage


## Secretariat outline (E/CN.4/AC.1/3) → Cassin draft (E/CN.4/AC.1/W.2/Rev.2)

- 1. **replaced**: “or” → “and” (… interest of national welfare and security there shall be …)
- 2. **replaced**: “the right of emigration and expatriation shall not be denied” → “individuals may also freely emigrate or expatriate themselves” (… borders of each state individuals may also freely emigrate or expatriate themselves …)

## Cassin draft (E/CN.4/AC.1/W.2/Rev.2) → Drafting Committee (E/CN.4/21)

- 1. **dropped**: “subject to any general law adopted in the interest of national welfare and security” (… subject to any general law adopted in the interest of national welfare and security there shall be liberty …)
- 2. **added**: “this freedom may be regulated by any general law adopted in the interest of national welfare and security” (… borders of each state this freedom may be regulated by any general law adopted in the interest of national welfare and security individuals may freely emigrate …)
- 3. **dropped**: “also” (… each state individuals may also freely emigrate or expatriate …)
- 4. **replaced**: “expatriate themselves” → “renounce their nationality” (… may freely emigrate or renounce their nationality …)

## Drafting Committee (E/CN.4/21) → Geneva working group (E/CN.4/57)

- 1. **added**: “subject to any general law not contrary to the purposes and principles of the united nations charter and adopted for specific reasons of security or in the general interest” (… subject to any general law not contrary to the purposes and principles of the united nations charter and adopted for specific reasons of security or in the general interest there shall be liberty …)
- 2. **replaced**: “this freedom may be regulated by any general law adopted in” → “individuals shall have” (… borders of each state individuals shall have the right to leave …)
- 3. **replaced**: “interest of national welfare” → “right to leave their own country” (… individuals shall have the right to leave their own country and if they so …)
- 4. **replaced**: “security individuals may freely emigrate or renounce” → “if they so desire to change” (… their own country and if they so desire to change their nationality to that …)
- 5. **added**: “to that of any country willing to accept them” (… to change their nationality to that of any country willing to accept them …)

## Geneva working group (E/CN.4/57) → Commission, 2nd session (E/600)

- 1. **dropped**: “the” (… of security or in the general interest there shall …)
- 2. **replaced**: “borders” → “border” (… of residence within the border of each state individuals …)
- 3. **replaced**: “change their” → “acquire the” (… they so desire to acquire the nationality of any country …)
- 4. **dropped**: “to that” (… to change their nationality to that of any country willing …)
- 5. **replaced**: “accept them” → “grant it” (… any country willing to grant it …)

## Commission, 2nd session (E/600) → Drafting Committee, 2nd session (E/CN.4/95)

- 1. **replaced**: “subject” → “every one is entitled” (… every one is entitled to freedom of movement …)
- 2. **replaced**: “any general law not contrary to the purposes and principles of the united nations charter and adopted for specific reasons of security or in general interest there shall be liberty” → “freedom” (… one is entitled to freedom of movement and residence …)
- 3. **dropped**: “free choice of” (… liberty of movement and free choice of residence within the border …)
- 4. **replaced**: “border” → “borders” (… and residence within the borders of each state every …)
- 5. **replaced**: “individuals shall have” → “every one has” (… borders of each state every one has the right to leave …)
- 6. **dropped**: “their own country and if they so desire to acquire the nationality of” (… the right to leave their own country and if they so desire to acquire the nationality of any country willing to …)
- 7. **replaced**: “willing to grant it” → “including his own” (… to leave any country including his own …)

## Drafting Committee, 2nd session (E/CN.4/95) → Commission, 3rd session (E/800)

- 1. **replaced**: “every one is entitled” → “everyone has the right” (… everyone has the right to freedom of movement …)
- 2. **replaced**: “every one” → “everyone” (… borders of each state everyone has the right to …)

## Commission, 3rd session (E/800) → Third Committee (A/777)

- 1. **added**: “and to return to his country” (… country including his own and to return to his country …)

## Third Committee (A/777) → Adopted (A/RES/217(III))

No change.

# Article 17: what changed at each stage


## Secretariat outline (E/CN.4/AC.1/3) → Cassin draft (E/CN.4/AC.1/W.2/Rev.2)

- 1. **dropped**: “his right to share in the ownership of industrial commercial and other profit-making enterprises is governed by the law of the state within which such enterprises are situated the state may regulate the acquisition and use of private property and determine those things that are susceptible of private appropriation” (… to own personal property his right to share in the ownership of industrial commercial and other profit-making enterprises is governed by the law of the state within which such enterprises are situated the state may regulate the acquisition and use of private property and determine those things that are susceptible of private appropriation no one shall be …)
- 2. **replaced**: “without” → “except for public welfare and with” (… deprived of his property except for public welfare and with just compensation the state …)
- 3. **added**: “the state may determine those things rights and enterprises that are susceptible of private appropriation and regulate the acquisition and use of such property” (… and with just compensation the state may determine those things rights and enterprises that are susceptible of private appropriation and regulate the acquisition and use of such property …)

## Cassin draft (E/CN.4/AC.1/W.2/Rev.2) → Drafting Committee (E/CN.4/21)

No change.


## Drafting Committee (E/CN.4/21) → Geneva working group (E/CN.4/57)

- 1. **replaced**: “every one” → “everyone” (… everyone has the right to …)
- 2. **replaced**: “a” → “the” (… everyone has the right to own property …)
- 3. **dropped**: “personal” (… a right to own personal property no one shall …)
- 4. **added**: “in conformity with the laws of the state in which such property is located” (… right to own property in conformity with the laws of the state in which such property is located no one shall be …)
- 5. **added**: “arbitrarily” (… no one shall be arbitrarily deprived of his property …)
- 6. **dropped**: “except for public welfare and with just compensation the state may determine those things rights and enterprises that are susceptible of private appropriation and regulate the acquisition and use of such property” (… deprived of his property except for public welfare and with just compensation the state may determine those things rights and enterprises that are susceptible of private appropriation and regulate the acquisition and use of such property …)

## Geneva working group (E/CN.4/57) → Commission, 2nd session (E/600)

- 1. **replaced**: “everyone” → “every one” (… every one has the right to …)

## Commission, 2nd session (E/600) → Drafting Committee, 2nd session (E/CN.4/95)

- 1. **added**: “such” (… the right to own such property as meets the …)
- 2. **replaced**: “in conformity with” → “as meets” (… to own such property as meets the essential needs of …)
- 3. **replaced**: “laws” → “essential needs of decent living that helps to maintain the dignity” (… property as meets the essential needs of decent living that helps to maintain the dignity of the individual and …)
- 4. **replaced**: “state in which such property is located no one” → “individual and of the home and” (… the dignity of the individual and of the home and shall not be arbitrarily …)
- 5. **added**: “not” (… the home and shall not be arbitrarily deprived of …)
- 6. **replaced**: “his property” → “it” (… be arbitrarily deprived of it …)

## Drafting Committee, 2nd session (E/CN.4/95) → Commission, 3rd session (E/800)

- 1. **replaced**: “every one” → “everyone” (… everyone has the right to …)
- 2. **dropped**: “such” (… the right to own such property as meets the …)
- 3. **added**: “alone” (… right to own property alone as well as in …)
- 4. **replaced**: “meets the essential needs of decent living that helps to maintain the dignity of the individual and of the home and” → “well as in association with others no one” (… own property alone as well as in association with others no one shall be arbitrarily deprived …)
- 5. **dropped**: “not” (… the home and shall not be arbitrarily deprived of …)
- 6. **replaced**: “it” → “his property” (… be arbitrarily deprived of his property …)

## Commission, 3rd session (E/800) → Third Committee (A/777)

No change.


## Third Committee (A/777) → Adopted (A/RES/217(III))

No change.

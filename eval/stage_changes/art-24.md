# Article 24: what changed at each stage


## Secretariat outline (E/CN.4/AC.1/3) → Cassin draft (E/CN.4/AC.1/W.2/Rev.2)

- 1. **added**: “and to the knowledge of the outside world” (… of rest and leisure and to the knowledge of the outside world …)

## Cassin draft (E/CN.4/AC.1/W.2/Rev.2) → Drafting Committee (E/CN.4/21)

- 1. **dropped**: “and to the knowledge of the outside world” (… of rest and leisure and to the knowledge of the outside world …)

## Drafting Committee (E/CN.4/21) → Geneva working group (E/CN.4/57)

- 1. **replaced**: “every one” → “everyone” (… everyone has the right to …)
- 2. **dropped**: “a fair share of” (… has the right to a fair share of rest and leisure …)

## Geneva working group (E/CN.4/57) → Commission, 2nd session (E/600)

- 1. **replaced**: “everyone” → “every one” (… every one has the right to …)
- 2. **added**: “rest and leisure should be ensured to every one by laws or contracts providing in particular for reasonable limitations on working hours and for periodic vacations with pay” (… to rest and leisure rest and leisure should be ensured to every one by laws or contracts providing in particular for reasonable limitations on working hours and for periodic vacations with pay …)

## Commission, 2nd session (E/600) → Drafting Committee, 2nd session (E/CN.4/95)

No change.


## Drafting Committee, 2nd session (E/CN.4/95) → Commission, 3rd session (E/800)

- 1. **replaced**: “every one” → “everyone” (… everyone has the right to …)
- 2. **dropped**: “rest and leisure should be ensured to every one by laws or contracts providing in particular for reasonable limitations on working hours and for periodic vacations with pay” (… to rest and leisure rest and leisure should be ensured to every one by laws or contracts providing in particular for reasonable limitations on working hours and for periodic vacations with pay …)

## Commission, 3rd session (E/800) → Third Committee (A/777)

- 1. **added**: “including reasonable limitation of working hours and periodic holidays with pay” (… to rest and leisure including reasonable limitation of working hours and periodic holidays with pay …)

## Third Committee (A/777) → Adopted (A/RES/217(III))

No change.

# Article 9: what changed at each stage


## Secretariat outline (E/CN.4/AC.1/3) → Cassin draft (E/CN.4/AC.1/W.2/Rev.2)

- 1. **replaced**: “save” → “or kept in custody except in cases prescribed” (… of his personal liberty or kept in custody except in cases prescribed by law and after …)
- 2. **dropped**: “a judgment of a court of law in conformity with the” (… personal liberty save by a judgment of a court of law in conformity with the law and after a …)
- 3. **replaced**: “a fair public trial at which he has had an opportunity for a full hearing or pending his trial which must take place within a reasonable time after his arrest detention by purely executive order shall be unlawful except in time of national emergency” → “due process” (… by law and after due process every one placed under …)
- 4. **replaced**: “shall be protected against arbitrary and unauthorized” → “placed under” (… due process every one placed under arrest or detention shall …)
- 5. **replaced**: “he” → “or detention” (… one placed under arrest or detention shall have the right …)

## Cassin draft (E/CN.4/AC.1/W.2/Rev.2) → Drafting Committee (E/CN.4/21)

No change.


## Drafting Committee (E/CN.4/21) → Geneva working group (E/CN.4/57)

- 1. **replaced**: “every one” → “everyone” (… and after due process everyone placed under arrest or …)
- 2. **added**: “and to trial within a reasonable time or to release” (… he may be subject and to trial within a reasonable time or to release …)

## Geneva working group (E/CN.4/57) → Commission, 2nd session (E/600)

- 1. **replaced**: “everyone” → “every one” (… and after due process every one placed under arrest or …)

## Commission, 2nd session (E/600) → Drafting Committee, 2nd session (E/CN.4/95)

No change.


## Drafting Committee, 2nd session (E/CN.4/95) → Commission, 3rd session (E/800)

- 1. **replaced**: “deprived of his personal liberty or kept in custody except in cases prescribed by law and after due process every one placed under” → “subjected to arbitrary” (… no one shall be subjected to arbitrary arrest or detention …)
- 2. **dropped**: “shall have the right to immediate judicial determination of the legality of any detention to which he may be subject and to trial within a reasonable time or to release” (… under arrest or detention shall have the right to immediate judicial determination of the legality of any detention to which he may be subject and to trial within a reasonable time or to release …)

## Commission, 3rd session (E/800) → Third Committee (A/777)

- 1. **added**: “detention” (… subjected to arbitrary arrest detention or exile …)
- 2. **replaced**: “detention” → “exile” (… arbitrary arrest detention or exile …)

## Third Committee (A/777) → Adopted (A/RES/217(III))

No change.

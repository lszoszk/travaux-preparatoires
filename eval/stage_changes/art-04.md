# Article 4: what changed at each stage


## Secretariat outline (E/CN.4/AC.1/3) → Cassin draft (E/CN.4/AC.1/W.2/Rev.2)

- 1. **replaced**: “and compulsory labour are” → “which is” (… slavery which is inconsistent with the dignity …)
- 2. **replaced**: “and therefore” → “is” (… the dignity of man is prohibited public authority may …)
- 3. **added**: “public authority may impose a personal service or work only” (… of man is prohibited public authority may impose a personal service or work only by application of a …)
- 4. **replaced**: “this bill of rights but a man may be required to perform his just share of any public service that is equally incumbent upon all and his right to a livelihood is conditioned by his duty to work involuntary servitude may also be imposed as part” → “application” (… or work only by application of a law and …)
- 5. **dropped**: “punishment pronounced by a court of” (… as part of a punishment pronounced by a court of law …)
- 6. **added**: “and for the common interest” (… application of a law and for the common interest …)

## Cassin draft (E/CN.4/AC.1/W.2/Rev.2) → Drafting Committee (E/CN.4/21)

- 1. **replaced**: “public authority may impose a personal service or work only by application of a law and for the common interest” → “in all its forms” (… of man is prohibited in all its forms …)

## Drafting Committee (E/CN.4/21) → Geneva working group (E/CN.4/57)

- 1. **replaced**: “which is” → “in all its forms being” (… slavery in all its forms being inconsistent with the dignity …)
- 2. **replaced**: “is” → “shall be” (… the dignity of man shall be prohibited by law …)
- 3. **replaced**: “in all its forms” → “by law” (… man shall be prohibited by law …)

## Geneva working group (E/CN.4/57) → Commission, 2nd session (E/600)

No change.


## Commission, 2nd session (E/600) → Drafting Committee, 2nd session (E/CN.4/95)

- 1. **added**: “is prohibited” (… slavery is prohibited in all its forms …)
- 2. **dropped**: “being inconsistent with the dignity of man shall be prohibited by law” (… in all its forms being inconsistent with the dignity of man shall be prohibited by law …)

## Drafting Committee, 2nd session (E/CN.4/95) → Commission, 3rd session (E/800)

- 1. **added**: “no one shall be held in” (… no one shall be held in slavery or involuntary servitude …)
- 2. **replaced**: “is prohibited in all its forms” → “or involuntary servitude no one shall be subjected to torture or to cruel inhuman or degrading treatment or punishment” (… be held in slavery or involuntary servitude no one shall be subjected to torture or to cruel inhuman or degrading treatment or punishment …)

## Commission, 3rd session (E/800) → Third Committee (A/777)

- 1. **dropped**: “involuntary” (… held in slavery or involuntary servitude no one shall …)
- 2. **replaced**: “no one” → “slavery and the slave trade” (… in slavery or servitude slavery and the slave trade shall be prohibited in …)
- 3. **replaced**: “subjected to torture or to cruel inhuman or degrading treatment or punishment” → “prohibited in all their forms” (… slave trade shall be prohibited in all their forms …)

## Third Committee (A/777) → Adopted (A/RES/217(III))

No change.

# Article 3: what changed at each stage


## Secretariat outline (E/CN.4/AC.1/3) → Cassin draft (E/CN.4/AC.1/W.2/Rev.2)

- 1. **dropped**: “this right can be denied only to persons who have been convicted under general law of some crime to which the death penalty is attached every one has the right” (… the right to life this right can be denied only to persons who have been convicted under general law of some crime to which the death penalty is attached every one has the right to personal liberty …)
- 2. **added**: “and to personal security” (… life to personal liberty and to personal security …)

## Cassin draft (E/CN.4/AC.1/W.2/Rev.2) → Drafting Committee (E/CN.4/21)

No change.


## Drafting Committee (E/CN.4/21) → Geneva working group (E/CN.4/57)

- 1. **replaced**: “every one” → “everyone” (… everyone has the right to …)
- 2. **dropped**: “personal” (… right to life to personal liberty and to personal …)
- 3. **dropped**: “to personal” (… to personal liberty and to personal security …)
- 4. **added**: “of person” (… to liberty and security of person …)

## Geneva working group (E/CN.4/57) → Commission, 2nd session (E/600)

- 1. **replaced**: “everyone” → “every one” (… every one has the right to …)

## Commission, 2nd session (E/600) → Drafting Committee, 2nd session (E/CN.4/95)

No change.


## Drafting Committee, 2nd session (E/CN.4/95) → Commission, 3rd session (E/800)

- 1. **replaced**: “every one” → “everyone” (… everyone has the right to …)
- 2. **dropped**: “to” (… the right to life to liberty and security of …)

## Commission, 3rd session (E/800) → Third Committee (A/777)

- 1. **added**: “the” (… to life liberty and the security of person …)

## Third Committee (A/777) → Adopted (A/RES/217(III))

- 1. **dropped**: “the” (… to life liberty and the security of person …)

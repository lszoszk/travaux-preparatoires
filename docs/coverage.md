Left out of the count: 582 records of the Covenants' meeting series whose first two pages show they are not about the Covenants (other items of the Third Committee's agenda). That verdict was read from the text layer of two pages, so it is a judgment, not a check.

Resolutions (A/RES) are not counted: their texts are adopted texts, not documents to fetch. The cited symbols include documents outside the drafting record, so this share is a floor for what a reader following a citation will not find.

The gap is the L-series (amendments and draft resolutions), which the lists do not name and the harvest did not fetch: the speeches cite them, but the text of the amendment itself is not here. Their sponsors are read from the records (the amendments view), not from the documents.
